Everything You Need to Know Before Dining or Ordering

We are open daily from 7:00 AM to 10:30 PM. Breakfast is served from 7:00 AM – 11:30 AM, Lunch from 11:30 AM – 4:00 PM, and Dinner from 4:00 PM – 10:30 PM. Our curated selection of quick bites and chef's small plates is available continuously from 11:00 AM until close for both dine-in table service and online delivery.
Guests enjoy complimentary, unmetered street parking directly along both sides of our block right outside the entrance. There are no parking meters or valet fees, making it effortless to stop in for a 15-minute quick bite or unload strollers for a relaxed family dinner.
Yes! Our dining room is intentionally designed with a casual, quiet acoustic profile (~42 dB) divided into thoughtful seating zones: cozy solo dining nooks with power outlets, spacious family booths with high-chairs and kids' small plates, and conversational group tables for 4 to 12 guests.
